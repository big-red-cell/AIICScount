Since they are called from Chrome, any attacker can simply attach a debugger or network monitor to Chrome and watch how the API calls are made regardless, and there&#39;s no practical way to prevent that. Therefore, the APIs being publicly undocumented is not a security boundary - rather, it is intended to prevent people from accidentally depending on APIs that have no support guarantees from Google.</p>
