ar...@chromium.org： <p>Hi Victor,</p> <p>Thanks for the additional details. I understand your concern about students accessing and potentially sharing the school Wi-Fi password through the sync-internals page. While this doesn&#39;t fall under Chrome&#39;s current threat model for web-accessible vulnerabilities, there&#39;s a way to address this using Chrome&#39;s admin policies: <a href="https://support.google.com/chrome/a/answer/7532419?hl=en" data-b-widget="url" data-b-original-href="https://support.google.com/chrome/a/answer/7532419?hl=en" data-b-redirected-href="https://www.google.com/url?q=https://support.google.com/chrome/a/answer/7532419?hl%3Den&amp;sa=D&amp;source=buganizer&amp;usg=AOvVaw12fhvXAl7NmC_cMH8STm6d">https://support.google.com/chrome/a/answer/7532419?hl=en</a></p> <p>Here&#39;s how to block access to the sync-internals page on school-managed Chromebooks:</p> <ol> <li>Go to the Google Admin console: Sign in with your administrator account.</li> <li>Navigate to Device Management: Click on Devices &gt; Chrome &gt; Settings &gt; Users &amp; browsers.</li> <li>Find the URL Blocking section: Under URL Blocking, you&#39;ll see two fields: Blocked URLs and Blocked URL exceptions.</li> <li>Add the sync-internals URL to the Blocked URLs list: In the Blocked URLs field, enter the following: <code>chrome://sync-internals</code> Click Save.</li> </ol> <p>Once this policy is applied, students will no longer be able to access the sync-internals page on their Chromebooks, preventing them from viewing the Wi-Fi password.</p> <p>Please note that this solution assumes the school Chromebooks are managed through the Google Admin console.</p> <p>+CC <a href="mailto:victorvianna@google.com" data-b-widget="url" data-b-original-href="mailto:victorvianna@google.com" data-b-redirected-href="mailto:victorvianna@google.com">victorvianna@google.com</a> as a Sync OWNER, as FYI.</p>
