hi...@chromium.org： <p>Thanks for reporting.</p> <p>I reproduced the issue on Linux, but Chrome looks working as intended:</p> <p>IIUC the patch is used to force triggering GC on every alert() call by <code>ThreadState::Current()-&gt;CollectAllGarbageForTesting();</code> and I observed that the GC destructs the <code>ClassicScript</code> during <code>V8ScriptRunner::CompileAndRunScript()</code>.</p> <p>However, the <code>CollectAllGarbageForTesting()</code> call uses <code>StackState::kNoHeapPointers</code> that ignores the <code>ClassicScript</code> pointer on the stack, which shouldn&#39;t occur in production builds. When I replaced the call with:</p> <p><code>ThreadState::Current()-&gt;CollectAllGarbageForTesting(ThreadState::StackState::kMayContainHeapPointers);</code></p> <p>that honors the on-stack pointers, Chrome didn&#39;t crash, because the on-stack <code>ClassicScript</code> pointer should keep alive the <code>ClassicScript</code> object.</p> <p>Also I replaced the <code>alert()</code> calls in the <code>poc.html</code> with <code>gc()</code>, and Chrome didn&#39;t crash as well.</p> <p>So I think the crash doesn&#39;t occur without the patch. Let me close the issue as WontFix, but please reopen it if you can still reproduce the crash without the patch or with <code>ThreadState::StackState::kMayContainHeapPointers</code>.</p>
