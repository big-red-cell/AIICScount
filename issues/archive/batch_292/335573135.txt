Typically, accessing the password manager through Google account settings mandates google passwords authentication but a simple click on the profile icon and selecting the  password manager (key logo) by passes the  authentication step, particularly on devices without a lock screen or hello interface. This presents a serious security risk, particularly in environments like schools or colleges, where multiple users may unintentionally leave their accounts logged in or in workplace potentially exposing sensitive information like passwords . Even I have been caught to accessing others important information  like passwords of a employee of  Nepal&#39;s central bank.</p> <h4>Please briefly explain who can exploit the vulnerability, and what they gain when doing so</h4> <p>I am writing to address a recent concern regarding the security vulnerability associated with the Google Autofill feature. This feature, which stores password and security key credentials in the Google database, has unintentionally introduced a significant weakness in our security system. The issue arises when a second party gains access to a device with Google Chrome installed, particularly if the device lacks password protection or any form of authentication. In such instances, unauthorized individuals are able to view all stored credentials without the need for any additional security measures.</p> <p>I have personally experienced the implications of this vulnerability. While testing the Autofill option, I was surprised to find that the system did not ask for any authentication. Further investigation across different devices confirmed the extent of this issue, showing the ability to access essential and sensitive information without proper authentication. However, I want to stress that I have avoided using this information in any harmful way. As you are aware, data privacy and security are paramount concerns for all users. The current setup, whereby passwords and security keys are automatically stored and readily accessible, poses a considerable risk to the confidentiality and integrity of our personal information and online accounts. To mitigate this risk and enhance our security posture, I respectfully urge the team to consider implementing additional security features. This could include the integration of two-way authentication processes and the introduction of a separate PIN requirement to access stored password credentials within the Google Autofill feature.</p> <p>Additionally, I have personally experienced the implications of this vulnerability.
