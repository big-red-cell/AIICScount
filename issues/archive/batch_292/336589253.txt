ja...@tamu.edu： I agree that a malicious attacker can always do worse things than this. The issue here is that the start_url should not be a path that attackers can exploit. For example, it performed well on your Desktop version; however, if attackers craft the start_url, they will bypass it. On an Android device, attackers can execute actions as described earlier. <br><br>The &quot;start_url&quot; should be within the &quot;scope,&quot; as defined by W3C, another field in the manifest file. However, crafting the value of &quot;start_url&quot; can cause it to fall outside of the scope, which is the crux of the problem here.<br><br>I believe it&#39;s not solely about what attackers can do; it&#39;s also about the violation of the least privilege principle. User privacy could be compromised if the Desktop and Android versions behave differently.<br>
