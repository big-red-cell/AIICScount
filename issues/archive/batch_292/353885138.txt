dr...@chromium.org： <p>[security triage] This is the intended behavior after <a href="https://crbug.com/40280033" data-b-widget="url" data-b-original-href="https://crbug.com/40280033" data-b-redirected-href="https://www.google.com/url?q=https://crbug.com/40280033&amp;sa=D&amp;source=buganizer&amp;usg=AOvVaw3azuclrsspcMJRyj2myo6h">https://crbug.com/40280033</a>. A user on abc.com is deciding to download a file based on their trust in abc.com. Especially for CDNs or blob URLs, they have never seen example.com. So it&#39;s not useful to show them a URL that does not communicate anything. Instead, we show them the referrer URL which they interacted with to get the download, which they have seen and are familiar with.</p>
