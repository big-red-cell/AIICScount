ma...@chromium.org： <p>[security shepherd]</p> <p>Confirmed that both cases reproduce in 128.0.6601.2 on macOS.</p> <p>In both cases a malicious extension opens a privileged Chrome page (the extension details page or downloads page) immediately behind an extension-controlled page. If the user interacts with that page in a specific way (double-clicking), the extension closes the page after the first click and the second click ends up directed to the privileged page (toggling an extension permission or opening a presumably malicious download respectively). The result is that the user can potentially be tricked into granting the extension extra permissions or executing downloaded files.</p> <p>Assigning to dbertoni@ based on extensions/OWNERS. Feel free to reassign if there is someone better suited to address this issue.</p>

This does present a risk of click-jacking (as well as other potential risks).
