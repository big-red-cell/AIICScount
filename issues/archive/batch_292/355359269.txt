The iframe document contains a link that, when clicked, opens a new window to a URL that executes script and submits a form.  Opening a new window is permitted by the sandbox. The new window inherits the iframe&#39;s sandbox. Executing script and submitting a form are both operations that are permitted by the sandbox inherited from the iframe.</p> <p>Removing <code>allow-scripts</code> or <code>allow-forms</code> from the sandbox causes the operations to be blocked.</p> <p>Closing as Intended Behavior.</p>
