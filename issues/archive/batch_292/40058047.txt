ym...@ym.kim： <p><strong>VULNERABILITY DETAILS</strong><br> The preview of <span>&lt;select&gt;</span> discloses whether the value is in the options, allowing inferring the state reliably from the preview and brute-force/de-anonymization attacks against other fields.</p> <p>Due to <a href="https://crbug.com/1075734" target="_blank">crbug.com/1075734</a>, the autofill fills fields of the same type up to 1000 times (effectively unlimited) for state fields, 19 for the credit card number, and 9 for others. However, this is still too large to prevent these attacks.</p> <p>AFAIK, some of the logic behind filling multiple times are<br> (1) confirmation fields: a <span>&lt;select&gt;</span> is rarely used, and they are not relevant in the preview<br> (2) synthetic/initially hidden fields, especially state fields: they won&#39;t be available/visible during the preview<br> (3) credit card number: a <span>&lt;select&gt;</span> is rarely used, and the masked number is displayed during the preview.<br> In addition, a <span>&lt;select&gt;</span> is rarely used for fields other than a state, country, and credit card expiration date. Therefore, the limit can be more restrictive during a preview for <span>&lt;select&gt;</span>, e.g., 3, with minimal impact.</p> <p>Furthermore, hidden <span>&lt;select&gt;</span> fields are filled during preview, which is invisible and unnecessary.</p> <p>Please find attached the suggested patch, which limits the number of <span>&lt;select&gt;</span>s of the same type filled to 3 and skips hidden <span>&lt;select&gt;</span>s for preview.</p> <p><strong>VERSION</strong><br> Reproducible on:</p> <ul> <li>96.0.4664.45 stable, Windows 11 Version 21H2 (Build 22000.318)</li> <li>98.0.4725.0 canary, Windows 11 Version 21H2 (Build 22000.318)</li> <li>96.0.4664.45 stable, Linux 5.11.0-36-generic #40~20.04.1-Ubuntu SMP</li> </ul> <p><strong>REPRODUCTION CASE</strong></p> <ol> <li>Add at least one U.S. address entry in chrome://settings/addresses</li> <li>Serve the attached poc.html and navigate to the page (an online version is available in <a href="https://me94bk1usdv8c8tfybvy.netlify.app/zosf6yiyiwfitg52tdiz.html" data-b-widget="url" data-b-original-href="https://me94bk1usdv8c8tfybvy.netlify.app/zosf6yiyiwfitg52tdiz.html" data-b-redirected-href="https://www.google.com/url?q=https://me94bk1usdv8c8tfybvy.netlify.app/zosf6yiyiwfitg52tdiz.html&amp;sa=D&amp;source=buganizer&amp;usg=AOvVaw3Fp2hCHOtmu4-mChvhuVIr">https://me94bk1usdv8c8tfybvy.netlify.app/zosf6yiyiwfitg52tdiz.html</a>)</li> <li>Click on any input and hover the mouse over an autofill entry OR press the up or down arrow key on focused input to preview autofill.</li> <li>The inferred content will appear below.</li> </ol> <p><strong>CREDIT INFORMATION</strong><br> Reporter credit: Young Min Kim (@ylemkimon), CompSec Lab at Seoul National University</p>
