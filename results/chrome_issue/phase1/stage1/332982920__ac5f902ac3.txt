Author: Hi Victor,

Thanks for the additional details. I understand your concern about students accessing and potentially sharing the school Wi-Fi password through the sync-internals page. While this doesn't fall under Chrome's current threat model for web-accessible vulnerabilities, there's a way to address this using Chrome's admin policies:
https://support.google.com/chrome/a/answer/7532419?hl=en

Here's how to block access to the sync-internals page on school-managed Chromebooks:

1. Go to the Google Admin console:
   Sign in with your administrator account.
2. Navigate to Device Management:
   Click on Devices > Chrome > Settings > Users & browsers.
3. Find the URL Blocking section:
   Under URL Blocking, you'll see two fields: Blocked URLs and Blocked URL exceptions.
4. Add the sync-internals URL to the Blocked URLs list:
   In the Blocked URLs field, enter the following: `chrome://sync-internals`
   Click Save.

Once this policy is applied, students will no longer be able to access the sync-internals page on their Chromebooks, preventing them from viewing the Wi-Fi password.

Please note that this solution assumes the school Chromebooks are managed through the Google Admin console.
