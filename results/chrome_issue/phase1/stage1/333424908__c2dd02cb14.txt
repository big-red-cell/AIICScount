Author: ja...@chromium.org
I looked a little bit closer. ThreadState::CollectedAllGarbageForTesting() was being called in poc.patch with no parameter, which amounts to an assertion that there are no oilpan pointers on the stack. There are quite clearly are at this point, and therefore objects on the stack are getting garbage collected.<br><br>That kind of garbage collection only happens in chrome&#39;s production code as part of an async task where it is safe to do so, I&#39;m comfortable saying this isn&#39;t reproducible without the patch.
