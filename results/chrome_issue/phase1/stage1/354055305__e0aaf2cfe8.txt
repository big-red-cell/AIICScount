<p>Hello,</p>
<p>I made this <a href="https://chromestatus.com/feature/5680742077038592" data-b-widget="url" data-b-original-href="https://chromestatus.com/feature/5680742077038592" data-b-redirected-href="https://www.google.com/url?q=https://chromestatus.com/feature/5680742077038592&amp;sa=D&amp;source=buganizer&amp;usg=AOvVaw3IhUBeRuL2tdhM83tXUpGJ">feature</a> in the past to avoid links to external application to escape sandbox.</p>
<p>See description:</p>
<pre><code>Block sandboxed iframe from opening external application.

To enable it again, apply at least one of following values to the &lt;iframe&gt; element&#39;s sandbox property:
- allow-popups
- allow-top-navigation
- allow-top-navigation-with-user-activation

Or use the new dedicated one:
- allow-top-navigation-to-custom-protocols
Motivation
If --as a site author-- developers sandbox untrusted content and don&#39;t give it the permission to navigate users away via normal navigations, they also don&#39;t want it to navigate away toward external app or the play store on top of their web page.

This problem was reported multiple time, by security researcher and companies wanting to get rid of malvertisers (malicious ads)
</code></pre>
<p>Since you used <code>allow_popups</code>, then it is working as expected.</p>
