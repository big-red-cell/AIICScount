<li>As described, the behavior involves a sandboxed iframe with the <code>allow-popups-to-escape-sandbox</code> attribute. This allows it to open a popup that does not inherit the sandbox restrictions.</li>
<li>Consequently, the popup can then open a modal window, which is the behavior you observed.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Conclusion:</strong></p>
<p>Based on the current web platform specifications and the intentional use of <code>allow-popups-to-escape-sandbox</code>, this behavior appears to be working as intended. The ability to open unrestricted popups from a sandboxed iframe is a documented feature, albeit one that requires careful consideration due to its security implications.</p>
