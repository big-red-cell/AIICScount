  <span class="stx-keyword">if</span> (g_bit) {

  }

  <span class="stx-keyword">for</span> (<span class="stx-class">size_t</span> <span>i</span> = <span class="stx-number">0</span>; <span>i</span> &lt; vp9-&gt;gof.num_frames_in_gof; ++<span>i</span>) {

    vp9-&gt;gof.num_ref_pics[<span>i</span>] = parser.ReadBits(<span class="stx-number">2</span>);              <span class="stx-comment">// [2]</span>

<p>However, in [1], the <code>vp9-&gt;gof.num_frames_in_gof</code> is a user-control <code>size_t</code>, which can be arbitrary value, and the access of <code>vp9-&gt;gof.num_ref_pics[i]</code> may be out of bound.</p>
