<h3>The problem</h3>
<h4>Please describe the technical details of the vulnerability</h4>
<p><strong>Summary:</strong>
A bug on YouTube allows ads to be skipped without being watched. This exploit occurs while an ad is playing, and it can be bypassed by following a simple step, allowing users to avoid watching ads altogether. The issue was discovered approximately two months ago and has not been fixed yet.</p>
<p><strong>Steps to Reproduce:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Click on a video and wait for an ad to start playing.</li>
<li>While the ad is playing, click on any &quot;Short Video&quot; on the YouTube page.</li>
<li>While watching the &quot;Short Video,&quot; immediately press the back button to return to the original video.</li>
<li>Upon returning, the ad is skipped automatically, and the video starts playing without the ad being shown.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Expected Outcome</strong>: The ad should play in its entirety, and when returning to the original video after watching the &quot;Short Video,&quot; the ad should resume from where it left off.</p>
<h4>Please briefly explain who can exploit the vulnerability, and what they gain when doing so</h4>
<p><strong>Impact:</strong>
This bug poses a significant risk to YouTube’s ad revenue model. Since advertisements are a key part of YouTube’s monetization strategy, skipping ads directly results in revenue loss for both YouTube and the advertisers. Advertisers pay for their ads to be viewed, and if users can bypass ads easily, it diminishes the value of advertising on YouTube.</p>
<p>Additionally, if this bug is exploited at scale (e.g., via scripts, browser extensions, or automated tools), malicious users could systematically skip ads, leading to widespread disruption of YouTube’s ad delivery ecosystem. The bug also risks undermining the trust of advertisers, as they expect their ads to be seen, but this bug allows for effortless bypassing.</p>
<p><strong>Who can exploit the vulnerability, and what do they gain when doing so?</strong></p>
<p>Anyone who is aware of this vulnerability—whether casual YouTube users, malicious individuals, or even developers of browser extensions—can exploit it easily. No technical expertise is required, as the steps to trigger the bug involve simply navigating between videos using YouTube&#39;s interface.</p>
<p><strong>What they gain:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Ad-Free Viewing: Users can exploit this bug to watch videos without having to wait through or interact with ads, effectively receiving an uninterrupted viewing experience. This may encourage some users to avoid using ad-blockers or paying for premium services like YouTube Premium, which normally removes ads.</li>
<li>Financial Gain for Malicious Users: Developers could create automated tools (such as browser extensions or scripts) that systematically exploit this bug, allowing large numbers of users to skip ads. Such tools could be distributed or sold, leading to potential profit for those exploiting the bug.</li>
<li>Potential Disruption for Competitors: In theory, someone looking to damage YouTube’s revenue could spread awareness of this bug to harm YouTube’s relationship with advertisers, as they would feel their ads are not being seen by users despite paying for them.</li>
</ol>
