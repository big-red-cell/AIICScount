Yes, it&#39;s true that the fullscreen mode provides a clear notification to the user indicating they are in fullscreen. However, in fullscreen mode, when the domain within the iframe changes, the view should automatically minimize.<br><br>This is crucial because users may believe they are interacting with a trusted domain like <a href="http://google.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow ugc" data-b-widget="url" data-b-original-href="http://google.com" data-b-redirected-href="http://google.com">google.com</a> in fullscreen, but after a few moments, they could be redirected to a phishing site. I believe that browsers should minimize the fullscreen mode when the iframe&#39;s domain changes, alerting the user that the domain has switched.
