Author: el...@chromium.org
<p>When computing the HMAC of an ONC EncryptedConfiguration, the HMAC is taken only over the ciphertext, not including the IV used for it. Since the IV is passed in the EncryptedConfiguration as well, an active attacker can modify the IV to change what the ciphertext decrypts to without breaking the authentication on the packet. The odds that the result will be a valid ONC UnencryptedConfiguration are remote, but this is still not good.</p>
<p>Refs: <a href="https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromium/src/+/main/components/onc/docs/onc_spec.md#encrypted-configuration" data-b-widget="url" data-b-original-href="https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromium/src/+/main/components/onc/docs/onc_spec.md#encrypted-configuration" data-b-redirected-href="https://www.google.com/url?q=https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromium/src/%2B/main/components/onc/docs/onc_spec.md%23encrypted-configuration&amp;sa=D&amp;source=buganizer&amp;usg=AOvVaw1H4K3IziD3LHLmQwRt9CaF">ONC spec</a> (which requires this behavior), <a href="https://source.chromium.org/chromium/chromium/src/+/main:chromeos/components/onc/onc_utils.cc;l=712" data-b-widget="url" data-b-original-href="https://source.chromium.org/chromium/chromium/src/+/main:chromeos/components/onc/onc_utils.cc;l=712" data-b-redirected-href="https://www.google.com/url?q=https://source.chromium.org/chromium/chromium/src/%2B/main:chromeos/components/onc/onc_utils.cc;l%3D712&amp;sa=D&amp;source=buganizer&amp;usg=AOvVaw3zpW9DNUvxeZFUE2WextLW">our implementation</a>.</p>
<p>It is not obvious how this could be fixed since the behavior is baked into the spec and it&#39;s unlikely we can deploy a new version with an incompatible crypto layer. Also, the HMAC is HMAC-SHA1 anyway, which is not such a good crypto primitive...</p>
