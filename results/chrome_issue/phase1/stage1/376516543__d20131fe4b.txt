Author: jr...@google.com
<p>This is due to the use of <code>override</code> in the shader. These types of variable are not yet supported in the Tint IR, and so the crash is expected. We are currently working on adding support (see <a href="https://crbug.com/374971092" data-b-widget="url" data-b-original-href="https://crbug.com/374971092" data-b-redirected-href="https://www.google.com/url?q=https://crbug.com/374971092&amp;sa=D&amp;source=buganizer&amp;usg=AOvVaw2TQVOsDZatnYkbOrPoz7vr">crbug.com/374971092</a>).</p>
<p>This crash is not reachable from productions flows (i.e. Chrome), as <code>override</code> variables are replaced with constants before we convert shaders to Tint IR. As such, there is no vulnerability here.</p>
<p>I&#39;ll close this as &quot;wont fix&quot;, as this functionality gap is known and being actively worked on.</p>
