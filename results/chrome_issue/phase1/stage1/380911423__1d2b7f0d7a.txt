<p>If you run your iframe.html with the developer tools open you&#39;ll notice you get the following message:</p>
<p>&quot;Not allowed to navigate top frame to data URL: data:text/html,<span>&lt;script&gt;</span>alert();<span>&lt;/script&gt;</span>&quot;</p>
<p>This explains why you see the different behavior.  Navigating to data URL is permitted within an iframe but forbidden specifically and only within top frames.</p>
<p>Thus this is working-as-intended and not a security bug.  Closing this out.</p>
