<h3>The problem</h3>
<h4>Please describe the technical details of the vulnerability</h4>
<p>Hello Team,
I hope you are doing well.</p>
<p>There is a option in chrome setting that you can block the pop-us and redirects by choosing option <strong>Don&#39;t allow sites to send pop-ups or use redirects</strong> in <strong>Pop-ups and redirects</strong> setting. I am able to redirect even after the setting for Pop-ups and redirects is Don&#39;t allow sites to send pop-ups or use redirects is enabled. I am able to redirect even after i have added url mail.google.com in Not allowed to send pop-ups or use redirects.</p>
<p>Step-to-reproduce:</p>
<ol>
<li>Open any email and then click on open in new window icon.</li>
<li>From new email in new window, click on print and you will get a pop that this is not allowed as the  <strong>Don&#39;t allow sites to send pop-ups or use redirects</strong> is enabled.</li>
<li>Now to bypass this issue, open the email and directly click on print icon and you will notice that the email will print in new window. This should not be allowed as <strong>Don&#39;t allow sites to send pop-ups or use redirects</strong> is enabled and no url is Allowed to send pop-ups and use redirects.</li>
</ol>
<p>Also i am able to use the print icon even after i have added url mail.google.com in Not allowed to send pop-ups or use redirects.</p>
<p>A video POC is attached as proof.</p>
<h4>Please briefly explain who can exploit the vulnerability, and what they gain when doing so</h4>
<p>By exploiting this vulnerability, the attacker can print mails by passing the Don&#39;t allow sites to send pop-ups or use redirects. Don&#39;t allow sites to send pop-ups or use redirects is enabled by the user but the redirect or pop-ups for print email is enabled.</p>
