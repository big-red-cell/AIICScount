<h1>Problem Description</h1>
<p>In <code>BackgroundThumbnailVideoCapturer::OnFrameCaptured</code>, there is no validation when
the progrma try to copy pixels via <code>installPixels</code>, i.e. there is no guarantee that
<code>mapping.memory().size()</code> is bigger than the <code>computeByteSize(row_bytes)</code>.</p>
<pre><code>  <span class="stx-keyword">void</span>* <span class="stx-keyword">const</span> pixels = <span class="stx-keyword">const_cast</span>&lt;<span class="stx-keyword">void</span>*&gt;(mapping.memory());

<h2>Threat Model</h2>
<p>We assume the adversary have a compomised renderer, so they can craft the Mojo call
and send a ``::media::mojom::VideoBufferHandlePtr data<code>, where the </code>pixels` are too
small to holds the full `SkImageInfo`, subsequently the copy will lead to
OOB in browser process. (From <a href="https://issues.chromium.org/issues/40063362" data-b-widget="issue-reference" data-b-issue-id="40063362">b/40063362</a> I assume the `::media::mojom::VideoBuffer*`
can be crafted by a compomised renderer process.)</p>
<p>To sum up, the attack model is attacker has a compomised renderer, and by exploiting
this vulnerbility, they may cause memory corruption, and potentially
escape the sandbox to gain browser process privilege.</p>

<p>The function [2] will extract a subset of the frame, which reuse the pixels
and take a subset of pixels. This cropped_frame&#39;s pixels might fall in<br>
[pixels, pixels + image_info.computeByteSize(row_bytes)), and the patch simluate the
scenarios where <code>image_info</code> is crafted, leading to <code>image_info.computeByteSize(row_bytes)</code>
bigger than the <code>mapping.size()</code>, subsequently, the cropped_frame&#39;s pixels
can be set to <code>pixels + mapping.size() + arbitrary OOB offset</code>.
Further causing the OOB in browser process. We simulate the pixels being
pointed to an invalid area at [3-4]</p>
