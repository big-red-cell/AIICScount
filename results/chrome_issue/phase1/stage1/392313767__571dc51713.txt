<h3>The problem</h3>
<h4>Please describe the technical details of the vulnerability</h4>
<p>Spoofing vulnerability has been identified in ios/iphone google chrome  where the browser misrepresents the data submission origin when an iframe is used to load a form. Specifically, when a form hosted on test.com is embedded in an iframe on abc.com, the browser pop-up misleadingly indicates that the form data is being submitted to abc.com, whereas the data is actually sent to test.com. This behavior violates user trust, creates origin misrepresentation, and can facilitate phishing attacks or data theft scenarios.</p>

<h4>Please briefly explain who can exploit the vulnerability, and what they gain when doing so</h4>
<p>An attacker who controls a website (e.g., abc.com) can embed an iframe containing a form from another domain (e.g., test.com) and leverage the browser&#39;s origin misrepresentation to trick users into believing the form belongs to abc.com.</p>
<p>Using this vulnerability, I was able to extract sensitive credit card information, including the card number and expiry date, from the iframe submission without the user&#39;s knowledge. This demonstrates the potential for attackers to collect payment details and use them for fraudulent transactions.</p>
