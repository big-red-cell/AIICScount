Security: brute-force/de-anonymization attacks against <select> autofill preview

Author: ym...@ym.kim
public_non_google

Author: **VULNERABILITY DETAILS**   
The preview of <select> discloses whether the value is in the options, allowing inferring the state reliably from the preview and brute-force/de-anonymization attacks against other fields.  
  
Due to crbug.com/1075734, the autofill fills fields of the same type up to 1000 times (effectively unlimited) for state fields, 19 for the credit card number, and 9 for others. However, this is still too large to prevent these attacks.  
  
AFAIK, some of the logic behind filling multiple times are  
(1) confirmation fields: a <select> is rarely used, and they are not relevant in the preview  
(2) synthetic/initially hidden fields, especially state fields: they won't be available/visible during the preview  
(3) credit card number: a <select> is rarely used, and the masked number is displayed during the preview.  
In addition, a <select> is rarely used for fields other than a state, country, and credit card expiration date. Therefore, the limit can be more restrictive during a preview for <select>, e.g., 3, with minimal impact.  
  
Furthermore, hidden <select> fields are filled during preview, which is invisible and unnecessary.  
  
Please find attached the suggested patch, which limits the number of <select>s of the same type filled to 3 and skips hidden <select>s for preview.  
  
**VERSION**   
Reproducible on:  
- 96.0.4664.45 stable, Windows 11 Version 21H2 (Build 22000.318)  
- 98.0.4725.0 canary, Windows 11 Version 21H2 (Build 22000.318)  
- 96.0.4664.45 stable, Linux 5.11.0-36-generic #40~20.04.1-Ubuntu SMP  
  
**REPRODUCTION CASE**   
1. Add at least one U.S. address entry in chrome://settings/addresses  
2. Serve the attached poc.html and navigate to the page (an online version is available in https://me94bk1usdv8c8tfybvy.netlify.app/zosf6yiyiwfitg52tdiz.html)  
3. Click on any input and hover the mouse over an autofill entry OR press the up or down arrow key on focused input to preview autofill.  
4. The inferred content will appear below.

<p>The high limit for state fields was chosen deliberately and not considered a security issue.</p>
<p>The suggested patch applies different limits for fill and preview. IIUC the report makes the case that most redundant state fields are be hidden anyway, so we can apply a small limit. Such distinctions between preview and fill can lead to surprising behaviour where a visible field is not previewed but filled.</p>
<p>That being said, the state can also be obtained by triggering a refill anyway.</p>
<p>Overall, I think this WAI.</p>
