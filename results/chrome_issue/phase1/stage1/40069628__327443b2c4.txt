In the feedback form, an attacker can insert the payload:` &quot;&gt;&lt;h1&gt;html injection test &lt;a href=<a href="https://www.google.com/url?q=https://example.com&amp;sa=D&amp;source=buganizer&amp;usg=AOvVaw32_WZYagLX5XuNCw35Xw40" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow ugc" data-b-widget="url" data-b-original-href="https://example.com" data-b-redirected-href="https://www.google.com/url?q=https://example.com&amp;sa=D&amp;source=buganizer&amp;usg=AOvVaw32_WZYagLX5XuNCw35Xw40">https://example.com</a>&gt;sjjsjsksksksksksk&lt;/a&gt; open redirect test &lt;img src=x&gt;nsksksksksagfsagasgasg.`  This payload can reflected on the page or app, leading to code execution. I have attached a proof-of-concept (PoC) video for better understanding.
