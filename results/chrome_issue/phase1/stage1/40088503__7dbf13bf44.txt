Author: As of https://codereview.chromium.org/1931063004, we're allowing `http://127.0.0.1/` to load in otherwise secure contexts, as we're ensured that traffic to that loopback address never transits the network.

I think I'd prefer to be consistent by implementing the above draft and allowing the requests for things other than web sockets, though.
