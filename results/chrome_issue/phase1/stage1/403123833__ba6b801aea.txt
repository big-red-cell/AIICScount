<h1>Steps to reproduce the problem</h1>
<ol>
<li>Borrow the victim&#39;s device.</li>
<li>Inject the payload into the vulnerable field (Home Page button).</li>
<li>Wait for the victim to log in using the same browser and click the Home Page button.</li>
<li>The moment the victim clicks the button after logging in, their session cookie will be sent to the attacker.</li>
</ol>
<h1>Problem Description</h1>
<p>This vulnerability exploits Cross-Site Scripting (XSS) combined with social engineering to steal session cookies from unsuspecting users. By injecting a malicious payload into a vulnerable input field (such as the Home Page button), an attacker can manipulate the behavior of the application and extract sensitive user data.</p>
<h1>Summary</h1>
<p>Exploiting XSS with social engineering to steal session cookies via the Home Page button interaction</p>
