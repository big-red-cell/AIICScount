<h3>The problem</h3>
<h4>Please describe the technical details of the vulnerability</h4>
<p>Please describe the technical details of the vulnerability
V8 Sandbox Escape via Prototype Chain Access</p>
<p>Overview</p>
<p>This vulnerability exploits the Object. prototype and Realm objects of the V8 engine to access privileged properties or methods (such as detachGlobal) through the prototype chain.</p>
<p>Attackers attempt to obtain privileged objects or methods by manipulating the prototype chains of global objects, such as Object. prototype and Number. prototype.</p>
<p>Once a privileged method (such as detachGlobal) is found and called, attackers can break through V8&#39;s context isolation mechanism and achieve sandbox escape.
This escape may lead to access to global objects with higher privileges, or further exploit the implementation of remote code execution (RCE).</p>
