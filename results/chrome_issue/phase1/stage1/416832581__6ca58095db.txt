<p>The bug you reference (40055527) has two aspects to it:</p>
<ul>
<li>Download attribution is confusing, and downloads can be triggered over unrelated pages.</li>
<li>The origin displayed on the downloads list is wrong.</li>
</ul>
<p>The first issue is long-standing, and no longer considered a security bug (<a href="https://issues.chromium.org/issues/40055979" data-b-widget="issue-reference" data-b-issue-id="40055979">Issue 40055979</a>).</p>
<p>The latter issue is what caused the other bug to be rewarded, and that doesn&#39;t appear to apply here. At least, you don&#39;t demonstrate any issues with the origins shown on the downloads page.</p>
<p>As a result, I&#39;m closing this as working as intended.</p>
