<h1>Problem Description</h1>
<p>Chromium permits iframes to call <code>requestPointerLock()</code> without requiring <code>allow=&quot;pointer-lock&quot;</code>, and dangerously associates the permission with the top-level origin, not the iframe’s origin. This creates a spoofing vulnerability, where a third-party iframe can lock the pointer under the guise of a trusted top-level site, without the user&#39;s awareness or consent.</p>
<h1>Summary</h1>
<p>Security: Pointer Lock in iframe uses top-level origin for permission, enabling origin spoofing</p>
