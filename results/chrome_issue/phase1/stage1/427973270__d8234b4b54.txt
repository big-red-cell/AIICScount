<h3>Details</h3>
<p>[The below categories are suggestions intended to help you maximize the quality of your report (and your reward!). Please remove this hint and any unused categories before submitting your report.]</p>
<p><strong>Vulnerability Description</strong></p>
<p>Allowing camera/mic access persists allowing attackers to monitor users via their mic/camera. The only way with Chrome on iPhone to avoid this is to disable camera/mic completely for Chrome, there is no site by site option. Also to most users this is well hidden so they easily fall victim</p>
<p><strong>Attack Preconditions</strong>
Visit a site , navigate to a page that requests camera or mic access and accept
[Conditions that must be present to exploit this vulnerability (e.g. attacker needs permissions in victim&#39;s project; attacker needs to be in Bluetooth range of the victim; attacker needs to know a specific identifier; …)]</p>
<p><strong>Reproduction Steps / POC</strong></p>
<p>[Instructions on how to reproduce the issue, including <em>target/product information</em> and <em>reproduction output</em>]
Navigate to stripchat.com. Enter a models “room” showing live stream, request an exclusive private session and upon entering mic/camera access is requested</p>
<h3>Attack scenario</h3>
<p>Likely anyone with resources to create the site and lure  unsuspecting users to the attack vector</p>
