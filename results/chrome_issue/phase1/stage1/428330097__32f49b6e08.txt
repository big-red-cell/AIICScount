<h1>Problem Description</h1>
<p>By utilizing a redirector belonging to a trusted domain (e.g. youtube.com), the attacker can trick the browser UI into displaying a false download source, even though the file was downloaded from a third-party domain (malicious domain). This creates a very persuasive origin spoofing condition that is difficult for users to detect.</p>
