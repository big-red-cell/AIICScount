<h3>The problem</h3>
<h4>Please describe the technical details of the vulnerability</h4>
<ol>
<li>Open Chrome and navigate to localhost:8000/testcase.html</li>
<li>Move the browser window to the right so that part of the page is positioned off-screen.</li>
<li>Click the button on the page to focus it.</li>
<li>Press Tab twice then Enter</li>
</ol>
<h4>Impact analysis – Please briefly explain who can exploit the vulnerability, and what they gain when doing so</h4>
