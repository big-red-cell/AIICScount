<h3>The problem</h3>
<h4>Please describe the technical details of the vulnerability</h4>
<p>This vulnerability can be exploited in public places like computer stations at universities, or workstations. Even when the user has their google account logged out of a system, their saved passwords for other apps can easily be exploited. I came across this in my university, alot of students use the computers and most of them do logout from the systems, yet I was able to check their passwords. This is a security concern and I really hope this is brought to notice to be fixed. Below I will mention the steps I took to be able to see these passwords. I could possibly make a video and attach it later if I receive an email for this vulnerability as well.
Step 1: check if there are google accounts present in the system (it can be logged out it doesn’t matter, but there needs to be an email which shows you can sign in with password)
Step 2: click on the three dots on the top right corner
Step3: click on password manager
Step 4: Voila! if a person has saved passwords of other apps/websites on google you would have access to all the passwords.</p>
<p>I came across this randomly and I really hope to see this get fixed. I did not steal any sort of information from any of these accounts. I made sure to remove the account completely from all the computers that I worked with and found accounts on. People do not realise that they have to “remove” the account completely from a public system and this could lead to losses of a lot of their personal information.</p>
<h4>Impact analysis – Please briefly explain who can exploit the vulnerability, and what they gain when doing so</h4>
<p>I think anyone could easily exploit this vulnerability. By exploiting this, one can gain access to passwords, images saved on google drive, contacts and much more. I could send a demonstration video for more clarity in case my explanations weren’t the best.</p>
