<h3>The problem</h3>
<h4>Please describe the technical details of the vulnerability</h4>
<p><strong>Title:</strong>
Search history suggestions appear in the address bar of Google Chrome’s Incognito mode</p>
<p><strong>Description:</strong>
While using Incognito mode in the Google Chrome browser (version 138.0.7204.184 official, 64-bit), when typing the first letter in the search/address bar, suggestions based on previous searches made during private sessions are displayed. This indicates that the history from Incognito mode is being stored or accessed improperly, compromising the promised privacy.</p>
<p><strong>Steps to reproduce:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Open Google Chrome in Incognito mode.</li>
<li>Perform a search with a specific term.</li>
<li>Close the Incognito tab.</li>
<li>Open a new Incognito tab and start typing the first letter of the previously searched term.</li>
<li>Observe that the suggestion appears in the search bar.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Environment:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Browser: Google Chrome (version 138.0.7204.184 official, 64-bit)</li>
<li>Operating System: Windows 10</li>
<li>Device: Samsung Book E20</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Proof:</strong>
Attached is a video clearly demonstrating the described behavior.</p>
<h4>Impact analysis – Please briefly explain who can exploit the vulnerability, and what they gain when doing so</h4>
<p><strong>Impact:</strong>
This behavior breaks the expected privacy of Incognito mode, exposing searches made in previous private sessions and potentially causing issues in shared environments.</p>
