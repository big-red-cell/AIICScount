<h1>Steps to reproduce the problem</h1>
<ol>
<li>Install Adblock Plus</li>
<li>Wait for updates</li>
<li>Every update triggers a new tab with an ad campaign for paid premium services</li>
</ol>
<h1>Problem Description</h1>
<p>Extensions are abusing the automatic extension update system to constantly spam end users with their ads for their paid premium services because each update triggers an URL opening in a new tab and this cannot be stopped without deinstalling the extension. The chief contender is Adblock Plus, which is hiding its ads under &quot;daily updates&quot; when even the URL gives away it is an ad campaign: <a href="https://adblockplus.org/de/update?ipm-campaign-id=5Q&amp;ipm-device-id=9650fe07-8827-445d-aae0-211e24f88ad7&amp;s=ipmnt&amp;ca=AA%3D%3D&amp;DE_EXCEPTION=1" data-b-widget="url" data-b-original-href="https://adblockplus.org/de/update?ipm-campaign-id=5Q&amp;ipm-device-id=9650fe07-8827-445d-aae0-211e24f88ad7&amp;s=ipmnt&amp;ca=AA%3D%3D&amp;DE_EXCEPTION=1" data-b-redirected-href="https://www.google.com/url?q=https://adblockplus.org/de/update?ipm-campaign-id%3D5Q%26ipm-device-id%3D9650fe07-8827-445d-aae0-211e24f88ad7%26s%3Dipmnt%26ca%3DAA%253D%253D%26DE_EXCEPTION%3D1&amp;sa=D&amp;source=buganizer&amp;usg=AOvVaw3N89wASY-qsoPYoHaxhEj0">https://adblockplus.org/de/update?ipm-campaign-id=5Q&amp;ipm-device-id=9650fe07-8827-445d-aae0-211e24f88ad7&amp;s=ipmnt&amp;ca=AA%3D%3D&amp;DE_EXCEPTION=1</a></p>
<p>This is a real security vulnerability because apparently extensions can open any URL of their choosing automatically and there&#39;s no browser setting or extension to prevent this. It should be possible to prevent extensions from opening new tabs.</p>
<h1>Summary</h1>
<p>Extensions are abusing the automatic update system to open unwanted and potentially harmful URLs</p>
