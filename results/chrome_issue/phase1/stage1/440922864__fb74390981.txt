<h3>The problem</h3>
<h4>Please describe the technical details of the vulnerability</h4>
<p>Description:</p>
<p>The YouTube redirect endpoint (/redirect) is intended to display an interstitial warning page before sending users to an external website. This interstitial is designed to alert users that they are leaving YouTube and prevent phishing attacks.</p>
<p>However, in Firefox, the interstitial is bypassed entirely. Users clicking a YouTube redirect link are directly sent to the external site, whereas in other browsers (Chrome, Edge, Brave), the interstitial appears and the user must manually confirm the navigation.</p>
<p>This behavior is consistent in virtual machines and other isolated environments.</p>

<p>Remediation / Mitigation Recommendation:</p>
<p>Consistent Interstitial Across Browsers:</p>
<p>Ensure that the “You are leaving YouTube” interstitial is displayed in all supported browsers, including Firefox.</p>
<p>This prevents phishing attacks that rely on bypassing the warning page.</p>
<p>NOTE TO REVIEWERS:</p>
<p>Although this issue involves a redirect, it cannot be dismissed as a standard open redirect.
The key concern is the browser-specific bypass of YouTube’s interstitial warning:</p>
<p>Chrome, Edge, and Brave correctly show the “You are leaving YouTube” warning page.</p>
<p>Firefox directly redirects users to the external domain without any warning.</p>
<p>This behavior creates a phishing risk and represents a cross-browser security inconsistency, which should be considered for review even if no script execution or token leakage occurs.</p>
<h4>Impact analysis – Please briefly explain who can exploit the vulnerability, and what they gain when doing so</h4>
<p>Impact:</p>
<p>Phishing vector: Users may trust a youtube.com link but are redirected to an attacker-controlled domain without warning in Firefox.</p>
<p>Cross-browser inconsistency: Firefox users are exposed to a risk that other browser users are protected from.</p>
