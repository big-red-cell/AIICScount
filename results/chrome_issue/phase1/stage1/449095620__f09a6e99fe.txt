<h1>Problem Description</h1>
<p>An attacker-controlled page can retain a reference to a new window opened to a target host and later manipulate that window (navigate, inject, message). If the target listens to postMessage without origin validation or opens content in a way that ends up same-origin, attackers can cause phishing, UI takeover, forced downloads, or other high-impact attacks. Recommend rel=&quot;noopener noreferrer&quot;, strict postMessage origin checks, and server/client hardening.</p>
