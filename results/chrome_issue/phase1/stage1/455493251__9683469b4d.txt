<p>The behavior you are describing is not a security bug in Chrome.</p>
<p>Your first and second screenshots show different Chrome profiles. In the first screenshot, that Chrome profile is logged into 2 Google accounts, and you are viewing the Google Drive for the second account (/u/1 in the URL). In the second screenshot, the primary Google account you are logged into is different from in the first Chrome profile, and that happens to be the same account that is the second account in the first profile.</p>
<p>See <a href="https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/2364824" data-b-widget="url" data-b-original-href="https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/2364824" data-b-redirected-href="https://www.google.com/url?q=https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/2364824&amp;sa=D&amp;source=buganizer&amp;usg=AOvVaw1PbzGCcsoom1RKzuCO4q0S">https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/2364824</a> and <a href="https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/1721977" data-b-widget="url" data-b-original-href="https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/1721977" data-b-redirected-href="https://www.google.com/url?q=https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/1721977&amp;sa=D&amp;source=buganizer&amp;usg=AOvVaw0rRNlFTUoCl3PvOs7uDGk1">https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/1721977</a> for information about multiple Chrome profiles and about signing into multiple Google accounts in one profile.</p>
