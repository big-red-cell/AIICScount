<h3>The problem</h3>
<h4>Please describe the technical details of the vulnerability</h4>
<p>The open redirect vulnerability allows an application to redirect users to another page without proper validation. Attackers can exploit this flaw to send victims to malicious sites that appear to come from a legitimate domain. From there, users may be tricked into downloading harmful applications or files. This vulnerability is highly dangerous because it makes phishing attacks and malware distribution much easier.</p>
<h4>Impact analysis – Please briefly explain who can exploit the vulnerability, and what they gain when doing so</h4>
<p>Anyone who can craft a malicious URL—typically an attacker or threat actor—can exploit an open redirect vulnerability. By doing so, they can redirect users to phishing pages, malicious download sites, or pages designed to steal credentials. The attacker gains the ability to mislead users using a trusted domain as the initial link, increasing the likelihood of successful phishing, credential theft, malware installation, and further compromise.</p>
