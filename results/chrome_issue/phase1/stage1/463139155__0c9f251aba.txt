<h3>The problem</h3>
<h4>Please describe the technical details of the vulnerability</h4>
<p>Google Password Manager Bug (Description)
I discovered a possible issue involving Google Password Manager. Under certain conditions—such as when a device is marked as stolen through Find My Device or when the phone hasn’t been used for an extended period—it may be possible to view saved passwords even when the Google account itself is logged out.</p>
<p>This seems to happen on devices that have no lock-screen password, or in rare cases where someone is able to guess the device’s unlock method. As a result, anyone who sets their device as stolen in Find My Device and does not have a secure lock screen could unintentionally expose their saved passwords.</p>
<h4>Impact analysis – Please briefly explain who can exploit the vulnerability, and what they gain when doing so</h4>
<p>This vulnerability could be exploited by someone who steals the device to gain unauthorized access to accounts, loyalty programs, emails, and other sensitive data, potentially causing significant harm.</p>
