<h1>Problem Description</h1>
<p>Cross-origin iframes can force top-window navigation without user interaction (click/gesture), allowing redirects to arbitrary domains — negating iframe navigation protection and allowing phishing / open-redirect abuse.</p>

<p>Cross-origin iframe can navigate top-window without user-gesture → bypass top-navigation restriction</p>
