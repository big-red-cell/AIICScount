<h1>Problem Description</h1>
<p>During fuzzing with a custom d8 build using V8’s sandbox-fuzzing mode, I observed a reproducible crash in the V8 engine where the fast-path implementation of Array.prototype.concat triggers an AddressSanitizer failure:</p>
<p>ERROR: AddressSanitizer: memcpy-param-overlap</p>
<p>This occurs inside V8’s optimized elements-copying routines (CopyDoubleToDoubleElements), where memcpy is invoked with overlapping source and destination regions.</p>
<p>Build Gn Args:</p>
<pre><code>gn gen out/d8_asan_fuzz --args=&#39;is_debug=false is_asan=true v8_enable_sandbox=true dcheck_always_on=false target_cpu=&quot;x64&quot;&#39;
</code></pre>
<p>d8 args:</p>
<pre><code>d8 --fuzzing --sandbox-fuzzing --single-threaded --allow-natives-syntax --expose-gc poc.js
</code></pre>
<h1>Summary</h1>
<p>V8 Sandbox: ArrayConcat fast-path triggers memcpy overlap under concurrent array header corruption</p>
