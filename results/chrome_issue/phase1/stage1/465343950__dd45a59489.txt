<h3>The problem</h3>
<h4>Please describe the technical details of the vulnerability</h4>
<p>UI/UX Error Report – Right Panel Overlapping AI Search Results</p>
<p>Issue:
The right-side Google Business Profile panel is covering or overlapping the AI search results section on the left. This causes part of the AI-generated search content to be hidden or pushed behind the business popup.</p>
<p>Impact:</p>
<p>AI Overview content becomes partially invisible.</p>
<p>User may miss important information.</p>
<p>Layout appears broken or misaligned.</p>
<p>Reduces readability and disrupts search flow.</p>
<p>Technical Details:
The issue appears to be caused by an incorrect z-index, flex layout, or viewport width handling. The right panel is not respecting content boundaries, causing overlap on wider screens. Possible cause: fixed-position container without proper responsive spacing.</p>
<p>Device/Screen:
Desktop browser (Chrome, macOS).
Full-width view.</p>
<h4>Impact analysis – Please briefly explain who can exploit the vulnerability, and what they gain when doing so</h4>
<p>Impact Analysis</p>
<p>This vulnerability can be exploited unintentionally by any end-user, as no technical skill is required. When the interface hides or overlaps important information, users may be unable to view critical content, take informed actions, or correctly interpret system outputs. This can result in:</p>
<p>Misunderstanding of data displayed</p>
<p>Incomplete information shown during decision-making</p>
<p>Reduced usability and trust in the platform</p>
<p>Potential business or operational errors due to missing details</p>
<p>While this issue does not enable malicious activity or unauthorized access, it directly affects user experience, accessibility, and reliability of the interface.</p>
