<h3>Details</h3>
<p>After Chrome autofills a saved password into a login form, the password value becomes available in the DOM. By changing the input field type from &quot;password&quot; to &quot;text&quot;, the autofilled password is revealed.</p>
<p>This behavior may lead to accidental credential exposure on websites that do not properly secure autofilled password fields. Malicious or vulnerable client-side scripts on such websites could potentially access or display the autofilled credentials.</p>
