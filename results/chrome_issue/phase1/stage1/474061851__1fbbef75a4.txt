<h3>The problem</h3>
<h4>Please describe the technical details of the vulnerability</h4>
<p><em>hash fragments</em> or <em>GET parameter</em> in URLs can be used to inject prompts into the new AI assistant in the devtools of the chrome browser.
like that the AI can be tricked to reveal its entire systemprompt (see screenshot)</p>
<h4>Impact analysis</h4>
<p>everyone can trigger the vulnerability on basically every website.</p>
<p>it also can be used to:</p>
<ul>
<li>change the behaviour of the AI</li>
<li>to trick people in unwanted behaviour of the AI</li>
</ul>
<p>links can be shared with others so its not only a self exploitation (besides the systemprompt exfiltration) but also a  danger for others as the AI will change its behaviour and for example print malicious links and so on</p>
