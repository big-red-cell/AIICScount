Bug #150263: vino-server ignores hosts.deny and hosts.allow rules

URL: https://bugs.launchpad.net/ubuntu/+source/vino/+bug/150263

Metadata: security_related=true; information_type=Public Security; targets=vino (Ubuntu) (Won't Fix, Wishlist)

Description:
I am affraid that vino-server 2.20 ignores the hosts.allow and hosts.deny rules. 'ldd vino-server' shows no mention of libwrap which AFIK is essential for this support. This I think is a serious security flaw as it is currently virtually impossible to lock off the unwanted users from vino-server without using iptables for example.
