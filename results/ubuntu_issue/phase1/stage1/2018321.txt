Bug #2018321: rebuild of git-lfs on later golang version needed to remediate CVE-2022-32190

URL: https://bugs.launchpad.net/ubuntu/+source/git-lfs/+bug/2018321

Metadata: security_related=true; information_type=Public Security; tags=["cve-2022-32190"]; targets=git-lfs (Ubuntu) (Won't Fix, Undecided)

Description:
On ubuntu 22.04 the latest version of git-lfs is built on golang 1.18.1. This version of golang contains CVE-2022-32190, currently a FEDRAMP listed vulnerability.
https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2022-32190

I am requesting that git-lfs be rebuilt on a later version of golang that has the fix for this vulnerability, either 1.18.6+ or 1.19.1+
