Bug #2060389: Lock screen doesn't prevent media keys from working

URL: https://bugs.launchpad.net/ubuntu/+source/gnome-shell/+bug/2060389

Metadata: security_related=true; information_type=Public Security; tags=["amd64", "apport-bug", "jammy"]; targets=gnome-shell (Ubuntu) (Won't Fix, Undecided)

Description:
Steps to reproduce:

1) Open a music player e.g. Audacious with some music content
2) Lock the screen
3) Press Play/Pause media button (I'm using an Apple USB keyboard)

What happens:

Audacious starts playing music.

What should happen:

Nothing, because the screen is locked.

DistroRelease: Ubuntu 22.04
Package: gnome-shell 42.9-0ubuntu2
CasperMD5CheckResult: unknown
DisplayManager: gdm3
RelatedPackageVersions: mutter-common 42.9-0ubuntu5
